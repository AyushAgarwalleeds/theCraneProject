Wireless setup for installed receivers

Update an existing board here to keep its saved pairings and settings. Connect one board by USB. The setup report shows its addresses, Wi-Fi and recovery status.

  1. Register the handheld on each receiver. Join the receiver’s Wi-Fi and open http://192.168.4.1/. Open Settings and sender pairing, choose an empty sender slot, and enter the handheld’s station MAC. Generate a key. Keep the receiver station MAC and key available before tapping Save sender slot.
  2. Add that receiver on the handheld. Release all buttons. Hold Emergency Stop alone for eight seconds, then release to open handheld setup. Join its CraneLink-TX-… Wi-Fi, open http://192.168.4.1/, and enter the receiver’s label, station MAC, and the same key.
  3. Repeat for the other receivers. The handheld keeps the saved entries in order. Tap Finish and return to radio when done and disconnect your phone from receiver Wi-Fi. Reset Emergency Stop with Power + Stop for three seconds, then release both.

Master sender setup

      Receiver

      This setup replaces the receiver’s saved sender list.

      USB diagnostics

      Connect one board to see its Wi-Fi or ESP-NOW status. Power both boards during a radio check.

      Sender battery monitoring

      Connect a two-resistor divider to GPIO34 before enabling. Follow the battery, LED and buzzer wiring guide.

      Setup details

      Keep the Wi-Fi password and downloaded report private.

      Use the handheld and reset Emergency Stop
      • Power both boards with the phone disconnected from receiver Wi-Fi. Release every handheld button, then press a direction.
      • Hold Power On/Off for three seconds and release. Repeat to turn Power off.
      • On a master sender, tap Power, then immediately press and hold it for two seconds. Release it and count the LED flashes for the new receiver number.
      • After a stop, release all buttons. Hold Power and Emergency Stop together for three seconds, then release both. Press a direction again to test.
      • For phone control, join the receiver’s Wi-Fi and open 192.168.4.1. Disconnect from that Wi-Fi to hand control back to the handheld.
      USB troubleshooting
      • Close Arduino Serial Monitor and any other app using the COM port.
      • On Android, use a USB data cable or OTG adapter. Keep Chrome open while uploading. iPhone and iPad require a desktop browser for USB setup.
      • If the board cannot enter upload mode, hold BOOT, tap EN, then release BOOT and retry.
      • Use a classic ESP32-WROOM development board with at least 4 MB flash. ESP32-S3, C3 and other chips require different firmware.
      • Keep this tab open until setup finishes. If an upload succeeded but pairing failed, connect the same board and choose Retry pairing only.
